\section{Patterns and Properties\\ (Graphs of Derivatives)}
\subsection*{Goal}
The Learner will investigate graphs of derivatives and use their understanding
of graphs to develop strategies for finding the derivatives of trigonometric
functions.

\subsection*{Instructions}
\begin{itemize}
\item Find all the values of \(x\) for the graph of \(y=f(x)\) where
\(f'\) appears to be zero and mark the points on the \(x\)-axis.
\item Find all the values of \(x\) for the graph of \(y=f(x)\) where
\(f'\) appears to be one (1) and for each, mark the point \((x,1)\).
\item Find all the values of \(x\) for the graph of \(y=f(x)\) where
\(f'\) appears to be negative one (-1) and for each, mark the point \((x,-1)\).
\item Sketch a graph of \(y=f'(x)\) over the graph of \(y=f(x)\).
\item Repeat the above steps for \(y=g(x)\).
\end{itemize}
