Problems Involving Related Rates

  1. Change in Area for a Circle

    A circle is increasing in size over time in such a way that its radius is increasing at a rate of \(0.03\frac{\text{cm}}{\text{s}}\).

    At what rate is the area of the circle increasing when the radius is 33 cm?

  2. Dumping Gravel

    Gravel is being dumped from a conveyor belt into a pile at a rate of \(30\frac{\text{ft}^{3}}{\text{min}}\). It forms a pile in the shape of a right circular cone whose base diameter and height are always equal.

    How fast is the height of the pile increasing when the pile is \(19\) feet high?

  3. Speed of a Moving Spotlight

    A spotlight is shining on a wall. The spotlight is 10 feet from the wall and the angle between the spotlight and the ground is increasing at a rate of 0.2 radians per second.

    How fast is the spot of light moving on the wall when the angle is \(\frac{\pi}{4}\) radians?

  4. Filling a Horizontal Cylindrical Tank

    A cylindrical tank lies on its side, and it is being filled with water at a rate of 3 cubic feet per minute. The tank has a radius of 4 feet and a length of 10 feet.

    In a horizontal tank with radius \(R\) and length \(L\), when the water has a depth of \(h\), the volume of water in the tank is given by \[\begin{align}V&=\left(\frac{\pi }{2} - \arcsin\left(\frac{(R - h)}{R}\right)\right)LR^2 - L(R-h)\sqrt{\left(2Rh - h^{2}\right)}\\ &=\left(\arccos\left(\frac{(R - h)}{R}\right)\right)LR^2 - L(R-h)\sqrt{\left(2Rh - h^{2}\right)}\end{align}\]

    How fast is the water level rising when the water level is 2 feet deep?
