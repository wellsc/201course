Worksheet Exercise 2.

We will now find a formula for the volume of water in the tank as a function of time.
🔗

(a)

Recall that we can use similar triangles to find the relationships of the total height of the tank, the radius at the top of the tank, any depth of water, and the radius of the water at that depth.
🔗
Find the amount of water in the tank when we begin measuring. That is, when the height/depth of the water is \(2\text{m}\text{.}\) This is the volume at time \(t=0\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(V_{0}=\frac{8000\pi}{27}\ell.\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
From similar triangles, if \(r\) is the radius of the water when the depth is 2m, then \(\frac{r}{2\text{m}}=\frac{2\text{m}}{6\text{m}}\text{,}\) and so \(r=\frac{2}{3}\text{m}\text{,}\) and thus we have
\begin{align*} V\amp=\frac{1}{3}\pi{}r^{2}h\\ \amp=\frac{1}{3}\pi{}\left(\frac{2}{3}\text{m}\right)^{2}(2\text{m})\\ \amp=\frac{8\pi}{27}\text{m}^{3}\\ \amp=\frac{8000\pi}{27}\ell. \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

Use the volume at time \(t=0\) and the rate at which water is running into the tank to express the volume as a function of the time \(t\) (in seconds) since beginning to measure.
🔗
Your expression should have the form \(V=F(t)\text{,}\) where \(V\) is expressed in liters and \(t\) is expressed in seconds.
🔗
Answer.
\(V(t)=\left(\frac{8000\pi}{27}+20\pi{}t\right)\ell.\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

Given the initial volume of water, the total volume of the tank, and the fill rate, determine how long (after time \(t=0\)) it will take for the tank to be completely full.
🔗
Answer.
\(t\approx 385.185\text{s}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
We need to solve \(V(t)=V_{\text{total}}\text{,}\) or \(\left(\frac{8000\pi}{27}+20\pi{}t\right)\ell=8000\pi\ell\text{.}\) Thus,
\begin{align*} \left(\frac{8000\pi}{27}+20\pi{}t\right)=8000\pi \amp\longrightarrow \frac{1}{27}\cdot 8000\pi+20\pi{}t=8000\pi\\ \amp\longrightarrow \frac{1}{27}\cdot 8000\pi+20\pi{}t=\frac{27}{27}\cdot 8000\pi\\ \amp\longrightarrow 20\pi{}t=\frac{26}{27}\cdot 8000\pi\\ \amp\longrightarrow t=\frac{26}{27}\cdot \frac{8000}{20}\\ \amp\longrightarrow t=\frac{26}{27}\cdot 400\\ \amp\longrightarrow t=\frac{10400}{27}\\ \amp\longrightarrow t\approx 385.185\text{s} \end{align*}
