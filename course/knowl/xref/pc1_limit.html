Worksheet Exercise 1.

An object is moving in such a way that its position \(s\) (in meters) at time \(t\) (in seconds) is given by the function \(s\left(t\right)=8{t}^{2}-10t+100\text{.}\)
🔗

(a)

(i)

Set up, but do not evaluate, the limit definition of the derivative that would find the derivative of the function at \(t=1\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
\begin{align*} \lim_{t\to{}1}\frac{s(t)-s(1)}{t-1}&= \lim_{t\to{}1}\frac{8{t}^{2}-10t+100-98}{t-1} \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
🔗

(ii)

Clearly explain why the limit you set up in Part a will find the derivative of \(s(t)\) at \(t=1\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Using the two different ways we have defined the derivative, the limit to find \(s'(1)\text{,}\) the derivative of \(s\) at \(t=1\text{,}\) is
\begin{align*} s'(t) &= \lim_{t\rightarrow 1}\frac{s(t)-s(1)}{t-1}\\ \text{or}&\\ s'(t) &= \lim_{h\rightarrow 0}\frac{s(1+h)-s(1)}{h}\\ &= \lim_{h\rightarrow 0}\frac{s(1+h)-s(1)}{(1+h)-1}. \end{align*}
🔗
The expressions \(\frac{s(t)-s(1)}{t-1}\) for any value of \(t\) other than \(1\text{,}\) and \(\frac{s(1+h)-s(1)}{(1+h)-1}\text{,}\) for any non-zero value of \(h\text{,}\) are the average rate of change between the points \((1,s(1))\) and \((t,s(t))\text{,}\) or between \((1,s(1))\) and \((1+h,s(1+h))\text{,}\) respectively, so the closer \(t\) is to \(1\) or the closer \(h\) is to zero, the closer the average rate of change is to being an instantaneous rate of change.
🔗
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

Evaluate the limit you found in Part a.
🔗
Solution.
\begin{align*} \lim_{t\to{}1}\frac{s(t)-s(1)}{t-1} &=\lim_{t\to{}1}\frac{8{t}^{2}-10t+100-98}{t-1}\\ &=\lim_{t\to{}1}\frac{8{t}^{2}-10t+2}{t-1}\\ &=\lim_{t\to{}1}\frac{(8{t}-2)(t-1)}{t-1}\\ &=\lim_{t\to{}1}(8{t}-2)\\ &=(8\cdot 1{t}-2)\\ &=6. \end{align*}
or,
\begin{align*} \lim_{h\to{}0}\frac{s(1+h)-s(1)}{t-1} &=\lim_{h\to{}0}\frac{8(1+h)^{2}-10(1+h)t+100-98}{h}\\ &=\lim_{h\to{}0}\frac{8{(1+h)}^{2}-10(1+h)+2}{h}\\ &=\lim_{h\to{}0}\frac{8{(1+2h+h^2)}-8-10h}{h}\\ &=\lim_{h\to{}0}\frac{8+16h+8h^2-8-10h}{h}\\ &=\lim_{h\to{}0}\frac{6h+8h^2}{h}\\ &=\lim_{h\to{}0}\frac{h(6+8h)}{h}\\ &=\lim_{h\to{}0}(6+8h)\\ &=6. \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

Find an equation for the tangent line to the graph of \(s(t)\) from Question 1 at the point \(t=1\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
The tangent line goes through the point \((1,s(1))=(1,98)\) and has slope \(s'(1)=6\text{.}\) Using the point slope form, an equation for the tangent line is
\begin{equation*} y=6(t-1)+98. \end{equation*}
