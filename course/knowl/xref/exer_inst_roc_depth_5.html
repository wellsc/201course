Worksheet Exercise 2.

In the exercise, we will make use of the algebraic techniques we have developed to calculate the instantaneous rate of change for the function \(f(t)=\sqrt[3]{0.54t+8}=\left({0.54t+8}\right)^{1/3}\)
🔗

(a)

Write the difference quotient \(\frac{f(b)-f(5)}{b-5}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(\frac{\left({0.54b+8}\right)^{1/3}-\left({0.54\cdot5+8}\right)^{1/3}}{b-5}\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

Find an appropriate expression so that
\begin{align*} \left(\left({0.54b+8}\right)^{1/3}-\left({0.54\cdot5+8}\right)^{1/3}\right)\left(\text{\ldots ? \ldots}\right)\amp=(0.54b+8)-(0.54\cdot5+8)\\ \amp=0.54(b-5) \end{align*}
🔗
Answer.
\(\left({0.54b+8}\right)^{2/3}+\left({0.54b+8}\right)^{1/3}\left({0.54\cdot 5+8}\right)^{1/3}+\left({0.54\cdot 5+8}\right)^{2/3}\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

Multiply the numerator and denominator of the difference quotient in Task 3.4.2.a by the expression you found in Task 3.4.2.b and simplify the numerator.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} \frac{0.54(b-5)}{(b-5)\left(\left({0.54b+8}\right)^{2/3}+\left({0.54b+8}\right)^{1/3}\left({0.54\cdot 5+8}\right)^{1/3}+\left({0.54\cdot 5+8}\right)^{2/3}\right)} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
🔗

(d)

Note that for any \(b\neq{}5\text{,}\) \((b-5)\neq{}0\text{,}\) and so \(\frac{(b-5)}{(b-5)}=1\text{.}\) Therefore, for every \(b\) except \(b=5\text{,}\) your expression in Task 3.4.2.c is identical to the same expression with the \((b-5)\) in the numerator canceled by the \((b-5)\) in the denominator, and therefore their limits as \(b\to 5\) are identical.
🔗
However, the expression with \((b-5)\) canceled is continuous at \(b=5\) and so its limit as \(b\to 5\) is simply its value at \(b=5\text{.}\)
🔗
Evaluate this limit, and thus calculate
\begin{equation*} \lim_{b\to 5}\frac{f(b)-f(5)}{b-5}\text{.} \end{equation*}
This is the instantaneous rate of change for \(f\) at \(t=5\text{.}\)
