Lemma 19 The Arithmetic Triangle.

For any non-negative integer \(n\text{,}\) and any integer \(k\) satisfying \(0\le k \le n\text{,}\) the binomial coefficient, defined by \(\binom{n}{k}=\frac{n!}{(n-k)!k!}\) satisfies
  • \(\binom{n}{0}=1\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(\binom{n}{n}=1\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • for any integer \(k\) with \(0<k<n\text{,}\)
    \begin{align*} \binom{n}{k}&= \binom{n-1}{k-1}+\binom{n-1}{k}. \end{align*}
